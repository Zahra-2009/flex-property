ABOUT ME

Hello! I Am Zahra

Hello! My name is Zahra. I am from Karachi, Pakistan. I love photography, especially night photography. My favourite hobby is to walk in old streets and capture beautiful neon signs, old boards and glowing lights.

For me, a sign is not just a board. It is a memory. It tells us where we are, where we have been, and where we want to go. I want to save these beautiful old signs through my camera before they are replaced by new LED screens.

My dream is to make my own photo exhibition one day, full of neon lights, old cities and warm memories.
